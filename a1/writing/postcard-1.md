# A holiday postcard

A holiday postcard in English is short and cheerful. It says where the writer is, what the weather is like and what happens every day, then ends with a warm goodbye.

A postcard has little space, so every sentence carries one piece of news. The {term}`present tense` does most of the work: *The hotel is lovely*, *We swim every morning*. The greeting is casual, often *Dear* or *Hi* with the first name. The classic last line is *Wish you were here!*, a fixed phrase that every English speaker knows.

## Example: A week in Cornwall

Maria is on holiday in Cornwall with her husband. She writes a postcard to her colleague Jane at the office.

> Dear Jane,
>
> Greetings from Cornwall! We are in a small hotel by the sea in St Ives. The weather is sunny and warm, and the sea is beautiful. Every morning we swim, and in the afternoon we walk on the beach. The food is very good, and the fish is fresh every day. We come home on Saturday.
>
> Wish you were here!
>
> Love,
> Maria

The first line says where the card is from. Then come the place, the weather, the days and the food, one sentence each. The day of return is useful news for a colleague. *Wish you were here!* closes the card before the goodbye.

## Building blocks

The postcard frame, ready for any holiday:

| Phrase | Notes |
|---|---|
| {stack}`Greetings from …! \| Viele Grüße aus …!` | the first line, with the place |
| {stack}`We are in a … by the sea. \| Wir sind in einem … am Meer.` | where the writer is staying |
| {stack}`The weather is sunny. \| Das Wetter ist sonnig.` | also *rainy*, *cold*, *hot* |
| {stack}`Every morning we … \| Jeden Morgen …` | what happens each day |
| {stack}`The food is very good. \| Das Essen ist sehr gut.` | a short opinion |
| {stack}`We come home on … \| Wir kommen am … nach Hause.` | the day of return |
| {stack}`Wish you were here! \| Schade, dass du nicht hier bist!` | fixed last line |
| {stack}`See you soon, \| Bis bald,` | casual goodbye |
| {stack}`Love, \| Alles Liebe,` | warm goodbye |

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- Start with the place: *Greetings from Cornwall!*
- One sentence each for the place, the weather, the days and the food.
- The present tense carries the news: *We swim every morning.*
- Finish with *Wish you were here!* and *Love* or *See you soon*.
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*The weather is sun.* → The weather is **sunny**. (the describing word ends in *-y*)
- *\*We are swimming every morning.* → We **swim** every morning. (a habit uses the present simple)
- *\*Greetings of Cornwall!* → Greetings **from** Cornwall!
- *\*Wish you are here!* → Wish you **were** here! (a fixed phrase, learned as it is)
:::
