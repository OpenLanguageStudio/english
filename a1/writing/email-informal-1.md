# An email to a friend

An informal email to a friend in English has a simple frame: a casual greeting, a question, some news and a warm goodbye.

The register is the level of formality, and between friends it is casual. The greeting is *Hi* or *Hello* with the first name, and the goodbye is *Love*, *Best wishes* or *See you soon*. Short forms such as *I'm* and *it's* are normal here. The frame stays the same for any topic, so only the news in the middle changes.

## Example: News about a new flat

Sophie has a new flat in Bristol, and she writes to her friend Tom in Leeds.

> **Subject:** My new flat
>
> Hi Tom,
>
> How are you? I'm in my new flat in Bristol now! It's small, but it's very nice. The kitchen is new and there's a big window in the living room. My neighbours are friendly. I work near the flat, so I walk to work every day.
>
> Are you free in June? Come and visit me! Bristol is great in the summer.
>
> Write soon!
>
> Love,
> Sophie

The email opens with a question about Tom, then gives the news in short sentences. The invitation in the second paragraph asks for a reply. *Love* is a common goodbye between close friends, and *Best wishes* fits friends who are less close.

## Building blocks

The informal frame, ready to reuse with any news:

| Phrase | Notes |
|---|---|
| {stack}`Hi Tom, \| Hallo Tom,` | greeting, with a comma after the name |
| {stack}`Hello Anna, \| Hallo Anna,` | a little less casual than *Hi* |
| {stack}`How are you? \| Wie geht es dir?` | the usual first line |
| {stack}`Thanks for your email! \| Danke für deine E-Mail!` | when replying |
| {stack}`I'm in … now. \| Ich bin jetzt in …` | for news about a place |
| {stack}`Are you free in …? \| Hast du im … Zeit?` | to make a plan |
| {stack}`Write soon! \| Schreib bald!` | closing line |
| {stack}`See you soon! \| Bis bald!` | closing line |
| {stack}`Love, \| Alles Liebe,` | close friends and family |
| {stack}`Best wishes, \| Viele Grüße,` | any friend |

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- Open with *Hi* or *Hello* and the first name, then a comma.
- Ask a question before the news: *How are you?*
- Give the news in short sentences, with short forms such as *I'm* and *it's*.
- Close with *Write soon!* and *Love* or *Best wishes*.
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *Dear Sir or Madam* to a friend: formal greeting, wrong register; use **Hi Tom**.
- *\*How you are?* → **How are you?** (the {term}`verb` comes before *you* in a question)
- *\*I'm live in Bristol.* → **I live** in Bristol. (no *am* in front of another verb)
- No goodbye at the end: always finish with *Love* or *Best wishes* and the name.
:::
