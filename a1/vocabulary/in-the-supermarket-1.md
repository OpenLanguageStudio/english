# In the supermarket

Supermarket words in English fill the first shopping list: bread, milk, eggs, apples. This pack covers a weekly shop and the two kinds of food words it needs.

Some food words count. *An egg*, *two eggs* and *an apple*, *three apples* are each a {term}`countable noun`, with *a* or *an* and a {term}`plural`. Others do not count. *Milk*, *bread*, *rice* and *cheese* are each an {term}`uncountable noun`: they take no *a* and no *-s*, and they go with *some* instead. *A bottle of milk* and *a loaf of bread* turn them into something that counts.

## The words

| Word | Notes |
|---|---|
| {stack}`bread \| food made from flour and baked` | uncountable; *a loaf of bread* |
| {stack}`milk \| the white drink from cows` | uncountable; *a bottle of milk* |
| {stack}`cheese \| a solid food made from milk` | uncountable |
| {stack}`butter \| a yellow fat made from milk` | uncountable |
| {stack}`rice \| small white grains, cooked in water` | uncountable; *a bag of rice* |
| {stack}`an egg \| what a chicken lays` | plural *eggs*; *an* before a vowel sound |
| {stack}`an apple \| a round red or green fruit` | plural *apples* |
| {stack}`a banana \| a long yellow fruit` | plural *bananas* |
| {stack}`a tomato \| a soft red fruit for salads` | plural *tomatoes* |
| {stack}`a potato \| a vegetable that grows under the ground` | plural *potatoes* |
| {stack}`a biscuit \| a small, flat, sweet baked snack` | plural *biscuits*; American English *a cookie* |
| {stack}`a bottle \| a tall container for drinks` | plural *bottles* |
| {stack}`a basket \| a container to carry the shopping` | plural *baskets* |

## *Some* and *any*

*Some* goes with uncountable nouns and with plurals in a positive sentence: *some milk*, *some apples*. Questions and negatives usually use *any*: *Is there any bread?* and *We don't have any eggs.* *Tomato* and *potato* add *-es* in the plural, not just *-s*.

## Summary

The pack fills a weekly shopping list. *Bread*, *milk*, *cheese*, *butter* and *rice* are uncountable and take *some*. *Egg*, *apple*, *banana*, *tomato*, *potato* and *biscuit* count, with *a* or *an* and a plural. *A bottle of* and *a loaf of* make the uncountable ones countable.
