# A1

A1 is the first CEFR level, for learners starting English from nothing. It covers the language of the first days in an English-speaking place: greetings, names, food and drink and the basic sentence.

## What learners know

- The articles *a*, *an* and *the*, and when to leave them out.
- The forms of *be* in statements, questions and short answers.
- The present simple for habits, facts and likes, with *do* and *does*.
- Words for food, the kitchen, clothes and colours.

## What learners can do

- Greet people and say goodbye, formally and casually.
- Introduce themselves and give basic personal details.
- Order food and drink in a café and ask for the bill.
- Write a short email to a friend or a simple formal email.
- Write a postcard about a holiday.
