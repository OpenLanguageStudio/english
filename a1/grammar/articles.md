# *A*, *an* and *the*

English has three articles: *a*, *an* and *the*. They come before a {term}`noun` and say whether it is new or already known.

The {term}`indefinite article` *a* or *an* marks one thing that is new or not specific: *a sandwich*. The {term}`definite article` *the* marks something specific or already known: *the sandwich on the table*. English articles never change for the noun, so *the* fits every noun, one or many. The choice between *a* and *an* depends only on the first sound of the next word. A general {term}`plural` or an {term}`uncountable noun` often takes no article at all.

## *A* or *an*: the first sound decides

*A* goes before a consonant sound, and *an* before a vowel sound. The spelling does not decide, the sound does: *an hour* starts with a vowel sound, *a university* with a *y* sound.

| Article | Before | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| a | a consonant sound | a cup, a house, a uniform |
| an | a vowel sound | an apple, an egg, an hour |

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
I'd like {article}`a` coffee.
: I would like one coffee, please.

She eats {article}`an` apple.
: She eats an apple.

We have {article}`an` hour.
: We have an hour.
:::

## *The* for specific or known things

*The* marks something mentioned before, the only one of its kind, or the one everyone can see. The first mention is often *a*, and after that it becomes *the*.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
I have {article}`a` dog. {article}`The` dog is called Max.
: I have a dog. That dog is called Max.

Where is {article}`the` station?
: Where is the station (in this town)?

{article}`The` sun is hot today.
: The sun is hot today.
:::

:::{note}
*A* and *an* only work with one thing. With a plural, *a* disappears or becomes *some*: *a cup*, *some cups*.
:::

## No article for things in general

A plural or an uncountable noun in a general statement takes no article. This holds for ideas too: *Life is beautiful*, never *The life is beautiful*.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
I like dogs.
: I like dogs in general.

Milk is good for children.
: Milk is good for children.

Life is beautiful.
: Life in general is beautiful.
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*She is teacher.* → She is **a** teacher. (a job takes *a*)
- *\*a apple* → **an** apple. (a vowel sound takes *an*)
- *\*an university* → **a** university. (it starts with a *y* sound)
- *\*The life is beautiful.* → Life is beautiful. (no article in general statements)
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- *A* before a consonant sound, *an* before a vowel sound: *a cup*, *an hour*.
- *The* for something specific or known, in every gender and number.
- First mention *a*, then *the*: *a dog*, *the dog*.
- Jobs take *a* or *an*: *She's a teacher*.
- General plurals and uncountables take no article: *I like dogs*, *Milk is good*.
:::
